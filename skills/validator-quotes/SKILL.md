# validator-quotes

The Muse launch was validated by technical founders and CEOs — Tobi Lütke
("looks very strong"), Patrick Collison, Ivan Zhao, DHH, Miguel de Icaza,
Garry Tan — whose audiences trust their judgment more than any brand
account. Borrowed credibility, deployed correctly, is the highest-leverage
launch asset there is.

## Who to approach

- **Technical founders and operators** whose followers are your buyers.
  Their quote carries weight because they could have built it themselves.
- **One per domain.** Commerce, dev tools, design, media: each validator
  unlocks a new audience. Five validators in the same niche is one
  validator five times.
- **People with a track record of honest takes.** A validator known for
  blunt criticism makes praise believable. A validator who praises
  everything makes praise worthless.

## Earning the quote

- **Give real access early.** A working product in their hands beats a
  pitch deck in their inbox.
- **Don't send talking points.** Scripted quotes read as scripted. Ask what
  they actually did with it and what surprised them.
- **Accept lukewarm.** "Looks very strong" from Lütke outperformed most
  press releases. A genuine mild positive beats a fake rave, and pushing
  for more burns the relationship.
- **Make it easy to say no.** The quotes you get from people who felt free
  to decline are the ones worth amplifying.

## Deploying the quote

- **Quote-post, one validator per post** (see `testimonial-launch` for the
  mechanics). Steady cadence over launch week.
- **Keep their exact words.** Don't polish, don't excerpt misleadingly.
  Their voice is the asset.
- **Tag and thank.** Small courtesy, large algorithmic and relational
  return.
- **Pair with the artifact.** Validator quote + the demo or outcome it
  refers to (see `mundane-demos`) is the complete unit.

## What not to do

- Never fabricate, embellish, or selectively edit a quote into a stronger
  claim than the person made.
- Never imply endorsement from a private conversation. If it wasn't public,
  ask before quoting.
- Don't stack all validators on day one. Spread them across the launch
  window to sustain momentum.
